Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers to help you evaluate rooftop solar investments in Nashik and Maharashtra.

System cost depends on capacity (kW), component specifications (tier-1 bifacial panels, European or high-tier string inverters), roof height, and net metering requirements. Turnkey residential systems typically range from ₹52,000 to ₹65,000 per kW before government subsidies. After applying the PM Surya Ghar subsidy (up to ₹78,000), a standard 3 kW home system effectively costs approximately ₹1,05,000 net.
The number of panels depends on your average monthly electricity consumption (kWh), panel wattage (we use high-yield 540W to 585W TOPCon modules), and shadow-free roof area. As a rule of thumb, a typical 3 kW system requires 6 panels (~240 sq.ft of roof), while a 5 kW system requires 9 panels (~400 sq.ft of roof).
A properly sized solar power system typically reduces grid electricity expenses by 70% to 90%. In Nashik's sunny climate, 1 kW of solar generates approximately 1,450 to 1,550 units (kWh) per year. With blended MSEDCL residential tariffs around ₹8.50 per unit, a 5 kW plant offsets over ₹60,000 to ₹65,000 of power bills every year, achieving full capital payback within 3 to 4 years.
Physical on-site installation of a residential rooftop system typically takes 3 to 5 working days. Commercial installations (20–100 kW) take 10 to 18 days. The subsequent MSEDCL net-metering inspection, bi-directional meter testing, and synchronization typically take 2 to 3 weeks.
Yes. Modern monocrystalline and TOPCon photovoltaic cells harvest diffuse ultraviolet and infrared light even under overcast or cloudy skies. While peak output is reduced to approximately 25%–40% on heavily rainy monsoon days, annual generation algorithms already account for monsoon variations across Maharashtra.
In a standard grid-tied net-metered system, your building automatically draws electricity from MSEDCL grid at night. The excess clean energy you exported into the grid during daytime hours is credited against your nighttime consumption via your bi-directional meter. If you install a hybrid battery system, nighttime power is drawn from your stored battery reserves first.
If your area has a stable electrical grid with minimal power cuts, an On-Grid Net-Metered system is the most cost-effective and highest-ROI option (no battery replacement costs). However, if your locality experiences frequent blackouts and you require seamless 24/7 backup for medical devices or IT equipment, a Hybrid Solar System with lithium-ion batteries is recommended.
Quality tier-1 solar panels are engineered to generate electricity reliably for 25 to 30 years. All our tier-1 modules come with a 10 to 12-year manufacturer product defect warranty and a 25-year linear performance warranty (guaranteeing at least 80% to 85% of initial power output after 25 years of continuous exposure).
Solar systems have no moving mechanical parts, resulting in minimal maintenance requirements. The primary maintenance is cleaning dust off the panel surface with water once every 10–15 days to maintain peak light absorption. Shaam Solar provides complimentary 1st-year AMC support with periodic electrical audits.
